# SOP — Instagram & WhatsApp Orders

**Owner:** Operations · **Applies to:** everyone who answers Instagram DMs or the
business WhatsApp · **Last updated:** 2026-10-17

Every order that starts in a DM or a chat ends up in the system. No notebook
orders, no "I'll enter it later". If it isn't in the admin panel, it doesn't exist.

---

## 1. Ground rules

1. **One business phone, one WhatsApp Business account.** Company-owned phone,
   screen lock on, WhatsApp encrypted backup on. Never take orders on a
   personal number.
2. **Never ask for or accept card details in chat.** Card payments happen only
   on our website. If a customer sends card numbers, delete the message and
   tell them to use the payment link.
3. **Bank transfers:** you may share *our* account details. The customer uploads
   the receipt on the payment link page, not in the chat. If they send a
   screenshot in chat anyway, upload it to the order and delete it from the phone.
4. **Message only people who contacted us or ordered from us.** No bought lists,
   no messaging numbers from comments, no forwarding chains. Promotions only to
   customers who ticked "send me offers".
5. **Never message the recipient before delivery when the order is a surprise.**
   Never send the recipient prices or offers. Recipients didn't sign up with us.
6. **Prices, delivery fees and cutoffs are what the system says.** You can't make
   up a discount. Use a coupon (ask a manager to create one) so it's recorded.

---

## 2. Taking an order (Instagram DM or WhatsApp)

| Step | What you do |
|---|---|
| 1. Greet & qualify | Find out: which product (ask for a screenshot of the reel/post), **delivery city**, **delivery date**. Check the product delivers to that city and the date is possible (the admin form will tell you). |
| 2. Collect details | Buyer name + WhatsApp number · recipient name + phone · full address + landmark · greeting card message · surprise or not · "from" name · payment method (COD or bank transfer). |
| 3. Enter the order | Admin panel → **New phone/DM order**. Pick the source (Instagram / WhatsApp), paste the IG handle or chat reference, and the reel/campaign name if known. |
| 4. Duplicate warning? | If the system warns that this buyer already has an order for the same recipient and date, **ask the customer** before overriding. |
| 5. Send the order link | On the order, tap **Send order summary**. WhatsApp opens with the message filled in. Press send. |
| 6. Customer confirms | The customer opens the link, checks the address, accepts the terms and (bank transfer) uploads the receipt. Until they confirm, the order is not final. |
| 7. Not confirmed in 6 h? | The order appears in **Messages to send** as a reminder. Send it once. Still nothing after 24 h → cancel the order and note "no response". |

Orders from the website's **Order on WhatsApp** button arrive with the product, option,
city, card message and link already in the first message. If it ends with `(ref: …)`, copy
that into **Reel / campaign**.

From Instagram: take the order in the DM if the customer prefers, but **send the
order link on WhatsApp**. If the customer won't use WhatsApp, paste the
link into the DM. The link is the same.

## 3. Payment

- **COD:** cash is collected at the door. Don't accept COD for **surprise** orders
  (the recipient would be asked to pay for their own gift). The system blocks it.
- **Bank transfer:** once the receipt is uploaded, a manager verifies it in the admin panel
  (**Verify payment**) against the bank app. Never mark paid from a screenshot alone.
- After payment is verified, send **Payment received** from **Messages to send**.

## 4. After the order

Open **Messages to send** at least **3 times a day** (10:00, 14:00, 18:00 PKT).
It lists every customer who should hear from us: order summary, payment
reminder, payment received, dispatched, out for delivery, delivered (with the
delivery photo link). It also lists messages **to the recipient** ("to the recipient"
in the list): "on its way" when the rider leaves (never for surprises) and, after
delivery, who the gift is from with their thank-you link. Never add prices or
order details to a recipient message. Pick **English** or **اردو** at the top (match the language the customer
writes in; the choice is remembered on your device). Tap → WhatsApp opens → send. The system records that you sent it, so
nobody sends it twice.

## 5. Changes and cancellations

| Request | Handling |
|---|---|
| Address/phone typo **before confirmation** | Customer fixes it on the order link page. |
| Address/date change **after confirmation** | Manager edits it in admin. If the shop has started preparing, ask the shop first. Note the change on the order. |
| Cancel before preparing | Cancel in admin. Refund bank transfers within 3 working days per the refund policy. |
| Cancel after preparing | Per refund policy (`/refund`). Escalate to a manager. |
| Product unavailable | Offer the closest alternative. Customer must re-confirm. |
| Custom design (photo cake etc.) | Attach the reference photo to the order notes. Price must come from the shop, never guessed. |

## 6. Opt-outs and complaints

- "Stop", "don't message me", or similar → reply once ("Done, you won't receive
  offers from us"), then tell a manager to turn off their marketing opt-in. You may still
  send messages about **their current order**.
- Complaints: acknowledge within 1 hour (working hours), escalate to a manager the same day,
  never argue in chat. Ask for photos.

## 7. Escalate to a manager

Refund or discount requests · payment disputes · threatening or abusive customers ·
suspected fake orders (new number, high value, COD, odd address) · anything
legal or press-related · a lost or stolen business phone (**immediately**: we log
out WhatsApp Web sessions and re-register the number).

## 8. Agent leaving the team

Remove them from WhatsApp Web linked devices and from the admin panel the same day.
Chats stay on the business phone, not theirs.
